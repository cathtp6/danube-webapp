新天鵝堡 Neuschwanstein Castle

新天鵝堡由路德維希二世下令興建，坐落於阿爾卑斯山麓高地，以白色外牆、尖塔、森林與山谷景觀聞名。它把中世紀城堡想像與十九世紀浪漫主義融合成童話般的建築；語音導覽可幫助了解建造背景、房間功能，以及國王對歌劇、傳說與理想王國的想像。

Commissioned by Ludwig II above the Alpine foothills, Neuschwanstein is known for white walls, towers, forests and valley views. It combines medieval castle imagery with nineteenth-century Romanticism; the audio guide explains its construction, rooms and the king’s fascination with opera, legends and an idealised kingdom.

加米施-帕滕基興 Garmisch-Partenkirchen

德國南部著名的阿爾卑斯山度假區，由 Garmisch 與 Partenkirchen 兩個歷史聚落組成。這裡以山景、傳統木屋、戶外運動與巴伐利亞文化聞名，也是通往德國最高峰楚格峰的重要門戶。城鎮適合作為山區活動前後的住宿、休息與補給基地。

A renowned Alpine resort formed from the historic settlements of Garmisch and Partenkirchen. Known for mountain scenery, traditional houses, outdoor sports and Bavarian culture, it is an important gateway to Zugspitze and an excellent base for accommodation, rest and supplies.

楚格峰 Zugspitze

楚格峰海拔 2,962 公尺，是德國最高峰，位於阿爾卑斯山脈。是否登頂需視時間、天候、纜車運行、交通與票券狀況決定；即使不登頂，從加米施-帕滕基興與艾布胡一帶也能欣賞壯闊高山景觀。

At 2,962 metres, Zugspitze is Germany’s highest mountain in the Alps. A summit visit depends on time, weather, cable-car operations, transport and tickets; even without reaching the top, the views from Garmisch-Partenkirchen and Eibsee are spectacular.

艾布胡 Eibsee

艾布胡位於楚格峰山腳，以清澈的藍綠色湖水、森林岸線與湖中小島著稱。湖水會隨天氣與光線呈現不同色彩，晴天時能映照阿爾卑斯山壁；湖畔步道適合散步與攝影，也可依時間安排遊湖或與纜車行程搭配。

Eibsee lies at the foot of Zugspitze and is known for turquoise water, forested shores and small islands. Its colours change with weather and light, and on clear days the Alps reflect across the lake. Lakeside paths are ideal for walking and photography, with boating or a cable-car visit possible depending on time.

帕特納赫峽谷 Partnach Gorge

帕特納赫峽谷是由帕特納河切割而成的狹長峽谷，岩壁高聳，瀑布與溪流穿行其中。步道沿著岩壁、橋道與隧道延伸，水氣、回聲與光影使短程健行具有戲劇性；建議穿著防滑鞋並預留安全行走時間。

Partnach Gorge is a narrow ravine carved by the Partnach River, with towering cliffs, waterfalls and rushing water. Paths run beside rock walls, bridges and tunnels, creating a dramatic short hike of spray, echoes and changing light. Wear grippy footwear and allow comfortable walking time.

帕紹 Passau

帕紹因多瑙河、因河與伊爾茲河三河匯流而被稱為「三河城」。老城坐落於狹長半島，巴洛克建築、河岸步道與聖斯德望主教座堂共同構成城市風貌；這裡也是本次河輪的登船與下船起點。

Passau is known as the “City of Three Rivers,” where the Danube, Inn and Ilz meet. Its old town occupies a narrow peninsula framed by Baroque buildings, riverside walks and St. Stephen’s Cathedral. It is the embarkation and disembarkation point for this cruise.

施洛格恩河灣 Schlögener Schlinge

多瑙河最具代表性的大彎道之一，河流在森林與山丘之間形成近乎迴轉的壯觀曲線。從船上觀看時，河道、坡地與晚間光線會隨航行角度變化。

One of the Danube’s most recognisable bends, forming a dramatic near-loop between wooded hills. From the ship, river, slopes and evening light change with the sailing angle.

梅爾克修道院 Stift Melk / Melk Abbey

梅爾克修道院正式名稱為「Abtei Melk」，是一座於 1089 年創立的本篤會修道院，修士在此已延續近千年的傳統。現今修道院約有 22 位修士，並持續依照本篤會規則生活與工作。訪客可參觀修道院圖書館、修道院教堂、修道院公園與介紹修道院歷史的博物館；今日所見的華麗巴洛克建築主要建於 1702 至 1746 年。內部參觀票價為每人 16 歐元，不含導覽。

Officially the Abbey of Melk, this Benedictine abbey was founded in 1089, and monks have continued their tradition here for almost 1,000 years. Today the community includes around 22 monks who live and work according to the Rule of Saint Benedict. Visitors can explore the library, abbey church, park and historical museum; the magnificent Baroque complex was built mainly between 1702 and 1746. Interior visit: €16 per person without a guide.

瓦豪河谷 Wachau Valley

瓦豪河谷是下奧地利多瑙河沿岸、梅爾克與克雷姆斯之間約 33 公里的景觀區。這裡以梯田葡萄園、中世紀城堡、修道院、巴洛克建築、河岸小鎮與杏果文化聞名，並於 2000 年列入 UNESCO 世界遺產。從船上航行時，可以看到葡萄園沿山坡層層排列，梅爾克、杜恩斯泰因與克雷姆斯等城鎮則將自然、農業與歷史建築連成一體。

The Wachau Valley is a stunning approximately 33-kilometre stretch of the Danube in Lower Austria between Melk and Krems. It is known for terraced vineyards, medieval castles, abbeys, Baroque architecture, riverside towns and apricot culture, and became a UNESCO World Heritage Site in 2000. From the ship, vineyards climb the hillsides while Melk, Dürnstein and Krems connect nature, agriculture and historic architecture.

霍夫堡皇宮 Hofburg Palace

霍夫堡皇宮訴說哈布斯堡王朝的歷史，曾是帝國長期的政治與生活中心。今日可參觀多座重要博物館，其中皇室珍寶館收藏帝冠、皇室器物與象徵王權的珍貴物件；Sisi 博物館則透過茜茜皇后的個人故事、服飾與生活細節，讓訪客看到皇室光環下較為私密的一面。宮殿群位於維也納市中心，適合與老城街區一併探索。

The Hofburg tells the story of the Habsburgs and once served as the empire’s political and residential centre. Today it includes major museums: the Treasury displays imperial crowns and regalia, while the Sisi Museum explores the empress through her personal story, clothing and daily life. Located in central Vienna, it pairs naturally with the old town.

匈牙利國會大廈 Hungarian Parliament Building

布達佩斯國會大廈，匈牙利語為 Országház，建於 1885 至 1904 年，是匈牙利國會所在地。新哥德式建築最初為匈牙利王國的國會而建，設計受到倫敦英國國會大廈的影響。建築沿多瑙河展開，長 268 公尺、寬 123 公尺、高 96 公尺，是匈牙利最高大的建築之一。白天可欣賞尖塔、立面與細部裝飾，夜間從河岸或船上觀看燈光倒映尤其經典。

The Budapest Parliament Building, Országház in Hungarian, was built from 1885 to 1904 and is the seat of the Hungarian Parliament. Originally designed for the parliament of the Kingdom of Hungary, its Neo-Gothic architecture was influenced by London’s Palace of Westminster. The building stretches 268 m along the Danube, is 123 m wide and 96 m high. Its illuminated façade is especially memorable at night from the river.

埃斯泰爾戈姆大教堂 St. Adalbert Cathedral / Esztergom Basilica

聖母升天暨聖艾德伯特主教座堂，也稱埃斯泰爾戈姆大教堂，是羅馬天主教埃斯泰爾戈姆—布達佩斯總教區的主教座堂，建於 1001 至 1010 年。它是匈牙利最大的教堂，也是世界上最大規模的教堂之一。巨大的圓頂、高聳立面與山坡上的位置，使它在多瑙河沿岸具有強烈的地標性。

The Cathedral Basilica of the Assumption of the Blessed Virgin Mary and St. Adalbert, also called Esztergom Basilica, is the cathedral of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Esztergom–Budapest. Built from 1001 to 1010, it is Hungary’s largest church and one of the largest in the world. Its huge dome, monumental façade and hillside position make it a powerful landmark above the Danube.

布拉提斯拉瓦老城 Bratislava Old Town

老城適合步行探索，街道兩側可見歷史建築、廣場、咖啡館、教堂與小型博物館。城市規模相對緊湊，能在有限靠岸時間內感受斯洛伐克首都與中歐多國歷史交會的痕跡；從老城向河岸延伸，也能欣賞多瑙河與現代城市景觀。

Bratislava Old Town is best explored on foot, with historic buildings, squares, cafés, churches and small museums. Its compact scale makes it well suited to limited shore time and reveals the capital’s Central European and multinational history; walking towards the river adds Danube and modern-city views.

杜恩斯泰因小鎮 Dürnstein

杜恩斯泰因是瓦豪河谷最具辨識度的小鎮之一，藍白色教堂塔樓、石造街道、葡萄園與山坡城堡遺跡共同構成典型的多瑙河景色。小鎮適合慢步散策、拍攝河景與建築；若時間與體力允許，可眺望山坡上的城堡遺跡，感受中世紀聚落與葡萄酒文化交織的氛圍。

Dürnstein is one of the Wachau’s most recognisable villages, where a blue-and-white church tower, stone lanes, vineyards and hillside castle ruins create a classic Danube scene. It is ideal for slow walks, river views and architecture; with time and energy, look towards the ruins above the village and experience the meeting of medieval history and wine culture.

慕尼黑啤酒節 Oktoberfest

慕尼黑啤酒節是世界知名的巴伐利亞啤酒與民俗節慶，除了大型啤酒帳篷，也包含傳統服飾、現場音樂、遊樂設施與地方飲食文化。下船後造訪，能為這段河輪與山區旅程增添熱鬧的終章；建議事先確認入場、座位、交通與行李安排。

Oktoberfest is a world-famous celebration of Bavarian beer and folk culture. Alongside the large beer tents, it features traditional dress, live music, rides and regional food. Visiting after disembarkation creates a lively finale; confirm access, seating, transport and luggage arrangements in advance.
